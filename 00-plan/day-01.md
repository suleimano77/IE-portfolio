## Block 1: Formulas that copy correctly (45 min)

Goal: build a sheet where you can drag one formula down and across and it stays right.

- Relative vs absolute references: `A1`, `$A$1`, `$A1`, `A$1`. Press F4 to cycle through them.
- Make a small multiplication table (1 to 12 × 1 to 12) using **one** formula typed once and dragged. If you can do that, you understand `$`.
- `SUM`, `AVERAGE`, `ROUND`, `MIN`, `MAX`, `COUNTIF`.

Try it first. Ask me only after 15 minutes of being stuck.

## Block 2: Logic (30 min)

- `IF`, then nested `IF`, then `IFS`.
- `AND`, `OR` inside `IF`.
- Exercise: a grade column that turns a score into A/B/C/D/F.

## Block 3: The deliverable (60 to 90 min)

Rebuild your problem set in Excel:

- One sheet per question.
- Inputs in coloured cells at the top. Every answer is calculated from them, with no numbers typed into formulas.
- Change one input and check every answer updates.

Save it as `01-excel/week01-problem-set.xlsx`.
